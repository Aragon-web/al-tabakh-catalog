I found the exact reason why your background color changes weren't showing up!

While your JavaScript (app.js) was successfully updating the body's CSS background variable, several large overlaid elements on the page (like the hero image overlay, the scrolled navbar, and the product hovers) had hardcoded black transparency values (e.g., rgba(13, 13, 18, 0.95)). Because these overlays covered most of the content, the new background color was hidden underneath them.

How I fixed it: I updated style.css and replaced all those static rgba definitions with the modern CSS color-mix() function, linking them directly to your dynamic CSS variables. For example: background: color-mix(in srgb, var(--clr-dark-base) 95%, transparent);

Now, whenever you change the bgColor, surfaceColor, or any other color in the Admin Panel, all of the glossy transparent effects and gradients on the site will automatically adapt to your chosen color, instead of staying black!
